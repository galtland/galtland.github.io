# Money and Banking

> Money is the most traded good in any economy and the one the state most wants to control, which is why it sits at the center of this wiki's argument. This hub follows the Austrian monetary arc from one end to the other: how a money arises on the market and what makes it sound, how banking and central banking generate the boom-and-bust cycle, how the fiat system that replaced gold works and what it is used for, and whether Bitcoin and privacy coins revive hard money in digital form. It is the monetary companion to [[austrian-economics|Austrian Economics]] — which supplies the method — and it shares its digital-money nodes with [[cypherpunk|Cypherpunk]].

## Where Money Comes From

The Austrian account of money is genetic: it explains money by how it arises, not by decree. [[carl-menger|Carl Menger]] showed that money emerges as a spontaneous order — traders accept the most *saleable* goods in indirect exchange until the most saleable of all becomes generally accepted, with no state required. [[ludwig-von-mises|Mises]] extended that account backward into a theory of money's *value* with the [[regression-theorem|regression theorem]]: money's purchasing power today traces through yesterday's back to the moment the good had only non-monetary worth — so no money can be conjured from nothing. Underneath both sits the [[subjective-value-vs-the-labor-theory-of-value|subjective theory of value]], and money's day-to-day purchasing power is set by [[cash-holding-and-the-demand-for-money|the demand to hold cash balances]] against its supply.

## Sound Money and Its Properties

If money can be more or less sound, what is the standard? [[hard-money|Hard Money]] collects the properties — scarcity, durability, divisibility, and [[fungibility|fungibility]] — that let a good hold value across time and remain interchangeable unit-for-unit. The historical instantiation of hard money is [[gold-standard|the gold standard]], whose discipline was precisely that it put the money's value beyond the reach of governments — and whose dismantling from 1913 to 1971 is the hinge of modern monetary history. The pricing of money over time runs through [[time-preference-and-interest|time preference and interest]], and the recurring question of whether a designed rule could deliver stable value is posed by [[ideal-money|Ideal Money]].

## Banking, Credit, and the Business Cycle

Banking is where money becomes elastic — and, on the Austrian view, where the trouble starts. [[credit-and-deferred-payment|Credit]] is the exchange of a present good for a future one; [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional-reserve banking]] lends out money depositors can demand back at once, a practice the [[hundred-percent-reserve-banking|100%-reserve]] school treats as inherent fraud. When banks and central banks expand credit, [[credit-expansion-dynamics|credit expansion]] pushes interest below its natural level, and [[austrian-business-cycle-theory|Austrian Business Cycle Theory]] traces the malinvestment boom and the corrective bust that follows — the mechanism [[rothbard-on-fed-induced-boom|Rothbard applied to the Federal Reserve]] and to [[great-depression|the Great Depression]]. The [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] is the institutional amplifier, and the [[cantillon-effect|Cantillon effect]] explains why new money is not neutral: those who receive it first gain at the expense of those who receive it last. Measuring the money that drives all this is itself contested — see [[monetary-aggregates-and-credit-expansion|monetary aggregates]].

## The Fiat System

Since 1971 the world has run on [[fiat-as-engineered-system|fiat money as an engineered system]] — money whose supply is a matter of policy, not mining. The wiki reads its instruments as a taxonomy of extraction: [[mises-on-capital-consumption|capital consumption]] as the deep effect of antiliberal policy, [[hoppe-on-caretaker-capital-consumption|the caretaker incentive]] that drives it under democracy, [[rothbard-on-wealth-tax|the wealth tax]] as a direct levy on accumulated capital, and — the newest instrument — [[hillebrand-on-cbdcs|central bank digital currencies]], concretely the [[digital-euro|digital euro]], which fold surveillance and programmability into money itself.

## Digital Hard Money

The wiki's distinctive claim is that the arc closes: [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is read as a candidate revival of gold's monetary discipline in software — hardness through a fixed supply, no counterparty, independence from any issuer — the [[unforgeable-costliness|unforgeable costliness]] gold had, now produced by [[proof-of-work|proof of work]]. Whether it truly succeeds gold is contested — the same regression-theorem question — and [[self-custody|self-custody]] is the precondition for the seizure-resistance it offers. Privacy coins push the sound-money project down to the unit: [[monero|Monero]] makes [[fungibility|fungibility]] mandatory. These nodes are shared with the [[cypherpunk|Cypherpunk]] hub, which treats them as tools of exit rather than as monetary theory.
